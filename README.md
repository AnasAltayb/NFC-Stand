# Axir Clinic - Smart NFC & QR Display Stand

A modular NFC and QR stand designed and manufactured for Axir Clinic to replace standard plastic display cards on the reception table. 

The physical stand routes patients directly to a custom digital portal hosted on GitHub Pages:
https://anasaltayb.github.io/Axir-Website/

---

## Technical Highlights

- Modular Insert System: The QR plate is designed as an independent body. If the digital link or QR pattern changes in the future, only the insert plate is re-printed instead of discarding the entire stand.
- Hardware Enclosure: A 2.0 mm internal clearance was engineered between the main body and the top cover to hold standard NFC tags securely without exposure.
- Print & Multi-Body Optimization: Split into 4 separate bodies before printing. This approach allowed optimizing the FDM print orientation for each part to improve surface finish and strength while avoiding costly single-plate filament swaps.
- Material Contrast: Integrated laser-cut gold plexiglas lettering for the "AXIR" logo directly into the 3D printed chassis.

---

## Manufacturing & Specs

- Main Body: High-Speed PLA (FDM Printed)
- Logo Inlay: Gold Plexiglas
- CAD & Surface Design: SolidWorks (Chassis) & Blender (Patterns and QR)
- Assembly: Cyanoacrylate bonding

## Deployment

The NFC tag and physical QR code link directly to the repository's root deployment on GitHub Pages, providing a lightweight front-end tailored for fast mobile loading.
